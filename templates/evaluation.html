How the measures are calculated

Appropriate reliance: accepting defensible recommendations and rejecting misleading recommendations.

Over-reliance: accepting a recommendation labelled misleading in the controlled dataset.

Under-reliance: rejecting a recommendation labelled defensible in the controlled dataset.

These measures use the reference assessments assigned to the fictional scenarios. They do not measure actual investment outcomes or establish causal effects. Results are descriptive and depend on the number and characteristics of submitted decisions.
